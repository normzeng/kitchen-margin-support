Menu Costing in Practice: Ingredients, Packaging, and Strawberry Milk

To calculate a menu item’s ingredient and packaging cost, multiply the unit cost of usable ingredients by the quantities used, then add the packaging for the order. Let’s use one cup of strawberry milk to check trimming loss and different packaging arrangements.

This costing example was written by the developer of Kitchen Margin. All prices and quantities are illustrative assumptions, not records from an actual café.

Strawberries use 90% usable yield and an 80 g after-trimming amount. Only the shopping-bag row is per order; every other row is per cup. For two cups, double the other quantities and add one bag.

1. Account for trimming yield first

If 1 kg of strawberries costs ₩9,000 and 900 g remains after removing stems and spoiled parts, the usable yield is 90%. The cost per usable gram is 9,000 ÷ 900 = ₩10. Using 80 g of trimmed strawberries therefore costs ₩800. Record the weight before and after trimming using the same scale. The trimming loss is already included, so do not add the same loss again.

2. Add the other ingredients

Using 180 mL from 1 L of milk costing ₩3,000 adds ₩540. Using 20 mL from 1 L of syrup costing ₩8,000 adds ₩160. Using 200 g from 1 kg of ice costing ₩250 adds ₩50. Ingredient cost is 800 + 540 + 160 + 50 = ₩1,550.

3. Separate packaging by order

A cup at ₩100, lid at ₩40, straw at ₩20, and sleeve at ₩30 total ₩190. One cup without a shopping bag costs ₩1,740. Do not apply disposable packaging costs unchanged to drinks served in reusable café cups. If two cups share one ₩100 shopping bag, the order costs ₩3,580 in total, or ₩1,790 per cup. A single cup in a shopping bag costs ₩1,840, so keep the order arrangements separate.

In practice, record recipes in reproducible units such as “80 g” rather than “two spoonfuls,” and check trimming and cooking losses. Calculate packaging unit prices from actual purchase quantities and total prices. Start regular price checks with ingredients you use often or whose prices change frequently. Choose a review schedule that suits your café.

All purchase prices include VAT. This calculation covers ingredients and packaging; it excludes labor, rent, payment processing fees, and other operating costs.

Follow along in the app

Set the currency to KRW. In Ingredients, save strawberries at 1 kg / ₩9,000 with a usable yield of 90%. In Items, create “Strawberry Milk (Takeaway)” and set “Servings per batch” to 1. For the strawberries, choose the amount measured after trimming and enter 80 g. Register the milk, syrup, ice, and packaging using the purchase quantities and total prices above, then enter their usage amounts. The cost for one cup without a shopping bag is ₩1,740. For a separate item representing two cups in one shopping bag, use 2 servings, double all ingredient and basic packaging quantities, and add 1 shopping bag.
