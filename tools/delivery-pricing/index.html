Why adding 30% to your menu price does not cover a 30% commission

Platform commission is charged on the new, higher delivery price—not your in-store price. If your dish costs $4.50 and your target food cost is 30%, you need $15.00 left after the platform takes its 30% cut:

Target Delivery Price (P*) = (C ÷ (T ÷ 100) + F ÷ n) × (1 + t ÷ 100) ÷ (1 − (c + f) ÷ 100 × (1 + t ÷ 100))

At 30% commission with 0% included tax, dividing $15.00 by (1 − 0.30) equals $21.43 ($21.43 × 30% = $6.43 commission, leaving $15.00, of which $4.50 is exactly 30.0%).
