Kitchen Margin is developed by Norman Zeng. This policy covers the Kitchen Margin app and this support website.

Effective September 25, 2026

At a glance

Your restaurant records and text recognition stay on your device. Kitchen Margin does not send that data to a developer backend and has no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. Apple processes subscriptions. You decide when to export or share files.

1. Information stored in the app

The app stores the information you enter or capture, including ingredients, recipes, prices, yields, allergen information, notes, production plans, sales and purchasing records, source images, recognized text, history, and settings. These records are kept locally on your device.

Text recognition uses Apple’s on-device technology. Source photos and recognized text are not uploaded to Norman Zeng or to a developer-operated recognition service. Kitchen Margin does not require a developer account or provide a developer-hosted database.

2. Permissions

Camera permission lets you photograph documents. The photo picker provides the images you choose for capture. The app uses those images to recognize text and retain the source with your records. You can manage permissions in your device’s Settings.

3. Subscriptions and Apple services

Kitchen Margin uses Apple StoreKit for subscriptions. Apple processes purchases, payment details, receipts, and transaction information under its own terms and privacy policy. The app receives subscription and transaction status from Apple to determine access to Capture features. The developer does not receive your payment card details and does not operate a subscription verification server.

Apple may separately process App Store usage, diagnostics, device backup, and account information according to your Apple settings and Apple’s policies. The app has no developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting service.

4. Exports, sharing, and device backups

You can choose to export complete backups, CSV files, and other outputs using system sharing tools. Exports may contain sensitive restaurant information, source photos, and personal information present in those photos. The destinations and people you choose may receive those files and handle them under their own policies.

Apple device backups may include app data depending on your backup settings. Kitchen Margin does not automatically sync your kitchen between devices or provide its own cloud backup. A subscription does not change that.

5. Retention and deletion

The app retains your kitchen data on the device until you remove it. Archiving a document keeps it available for history and restoration; archiving is not permanent deletion. Some deleted or replaced records may remain in historical snapshots or recovery copies stored by the app.

Deleting the app removes its local app data. To remove other copies, also delete any exported files, shared copies, and backups from the locations where they were saved, including Apple backups as appropriate. The developer cannot access or erase copies held on your device or in destinations you selected.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel it through Apple’s subscription settings.

6. Support and this website

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no developer-added analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or cookies. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to host and secure the service, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you choose to contact support through a GitHub issue, your GitHub username and everything you post are visible to the developer and to the public. We use that information to understand and respond to your request. Do not submit invoices, receipts, sales reports, backups, private business data, personal information, or payment information. Use fictional examples.

Public support discussions may remain available as a support record. You can edit your own issue content where GitHub permits, or request removal of a support post through the same support channel without reposting sensitive content. GitHub controls its own retention, account data, and platform copies under its policy.

7. Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app’s data practices change and show the effective date above. For questions about this policy, use the public support form; do not include private records or identifying information in your message.
