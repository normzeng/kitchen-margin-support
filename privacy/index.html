Privacy policy

Your kitchen.
Your records.

Kitchen Margin is developed by Norman Zeng. This policy covers the Kitchen Margin app and this support website.

Updated November 28, 2026

At a glance

Local-only costing and text recognition stay on your device. In versions with Shared Kitchen, you can choose to connect a kitchen and sync encrypted records. iPhone version 1.4 uses local records. The app has no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. Apple processes subscriptions. You choose when to export or share files.

The app also offers an optional “Share usage diagnostics” export in Settings → About: feature-use counts, event and installation timestamps, capture timings, coarse library-size ranges, and the app version, with no names, prices, photos, kitchen content, or direct identifiers. Nothing is sent automatically — you choose whether and where to share that file.

1. Information stored in the app

The app stores the information you enter or capture, including ingredients, recipes, prices, yields, allergen information, notes, production plans, sales and purchasing records, source images, recognized text, history, and settings. These records are kept locally on your device.

Text recognition uses Apple’s on-device technology. Original photo/PDF files are not uploaded to our account or recognition service. Local-only records stay on your device. In a linked kitchen, encrypted saved records can include recognized text and document metadata. Local-only use never requires an account.

Optional Shared Kitchen accounts

Black Vale Technologies LLC operates Shared Kitchen. You can use local costing and Capture without an account. When you choose Shared Kitchen, we use your account ID, display name, verified email address when available, and registered device names, IDs and public keys to manage access. Resend delivers email verification codes. Your passkey private keys and biometric information stay with your device or passkey provider.

You can choose email or any sign-in provider shown on your account screen. Google sign-in requests your basic profile and email address. We use your profile name when available and accept a verified Gmail or Google Workspace address. Shared Kitchen sign-in requests no Google Sheets access. From Facebook we request only the ID Facebook assigns to this app and your public profile name. Apple sign-in requests your email address, which can be a private relay address if you choose Hide My Email. We use this information to create, link and secure your account. We keep a hash of the provider identifier with your account and hold Google and Facebook tokens only in memory during verification. If you use Apple sign-in on iPhone or the web, we encrypt and retain a refresh token for each Apple sign-in client you use so we can revoke its authorization when you delete your account.

You can link a provider to your existing account after verifying its passkey. Opening a shared kitchen also requires an approved device with the kitchen keys. Connected devices encrypt records before sending them to private Cloudflare storage. Kitchen names, memberships, roles, approval records, revision times and proofs used to verify changes are readable service metadata. Cloudflare also hosts the account service.

In Settings, open Account and linked kitchens and choose Delete account. Transfer or delete a kitchen first if you are its last owner. Then type DELETE ACCOUNT and verify your passkey. Account deletion removes provider links and queues Apple authorization for revocation. Shared history can remain for other members. Local copies, exported files and subscriptions are managed separately. Read the account and shared kitchen privacy notice for recovery, retention and deletion instructions.

2. Permissions

Camera permission lets you photograph documents. The photo picker provides the images you choose for capture. The app uses those images to recognize text and retain the source with your records. You can manage permissions in your device’s Settings.

Optional recipe import

You can choose to import a recipe from pasted text, a screenshot, or a recipe page link. Choosing Load recipe link makes an HTTPS request directly to that website, including permitted redirects. The website receives standard network request information, including your IP address and the requested page address, under its own privacy practices. The app does not fetch recipe pages automatically or send your kitchen records to the website.

Recipe parsing and screenshot text recognition happen on your device. The importer does not send or store website cookies, use browser sign-in credentials, run page scripts, or load embedded images or other page resources. It does not send the page or recipe to a developer-operated parsing service. After you review and save imported records, the linked-kitchen sharing rules above apply.

When you continue to recipe review, the app keeps the original pasted text or downloaded HTML, the source page link when present, and the imported recipe text locally with the source record. These sources are included in complete app backups; Apple device backups may also include them depending on your settings. The sharing, retention and deletion rules below apply.

Optional Google Sheets import

Google Sheets import is an optional Capture feature currently available only to the approved owner test account. When you choose it, Google signs you in through its system authorization screen. Google asks for read-only permission to view all your Google Sheets spreadsheets. Kitchen Margin requests only the workbook link you enter and the worksheet you select. It does not list your Drive files, change your spreadsheet, or sync changes in the background.

The app sends Google the sign-in request and the spreadsheet identifier needed to read your selected worksheet. Google processes these requests under its privacy policy. The app keeps a short-lived Google access token in memory during the import screen and does not store a refresh token. It processes the selected worksheet on your device without sending Google tokens or the raw worksheet to the developer for parsing. Ingredient records you review and save can be included in optional encrypted linked-kitchen history under the sharing rules above. Disconnect Google clears the app’s access token. You can also revoke the app’s permission in your Google Account settings.

Only ingredient purchase details you review and import become local kitchen records. Imported prices are current ingredient prices, not historical invoices or purchase totals. The app remembers column numbers tied to a hash of the sheet, tab and headings on this device to reduce repeat mapping; it does not keep the original sheet contents in those preferences. Imported records follow the retention and deletion rules below.

Kitchen Margin's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use spreadsheet data to provide the import you request. We do not sell Google data or use it for advertising or AI training.

3. Subscriptions and Apple services

Kitchen Margin uses Apple StoreKit for subscriptions. Apple processes purchases, payment details, receipts, and transaction information under its own terms and privacy policy. The app receives subscription and transaction status from Apple to determine access to Capture features. The developer does not receive your payment card details and does not operate a subscription verification server.

Apple may separately process App Store usage, diagnostics, device backup, and account information according to your Apple settings and Apple’s policies. The app has no developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting service.

4. Exports, sharing, and device backups

You can choose to export complete backups, CSV files, and other outputs using system sharing tools. Exports may contain sensitive restaurant information, source photos, and personal information present in those photos. The destinations and people you choose may receive those files and handle them under their own policies.

Apple device backups may include app data depending on your backup settings. Local-only use and iPhone version 1.4 do not automatically sync kitchens. In versions with account linking, saved changes can exchange with an approved shared kitchen while the app is active and connected, including catch-up when you open or return to it. Offline work stays on its source device until it can be shared. Background push is not promised. Shared kitchens do not transfer original photo/PDF files or Capture access and do not replace complete device backups.

5. Retention and deletion

The app retains your kitchen data on the device until you remove it. Archiving a document keeps it available for history and restoration; archiving is not permanent deletion. Some deleted or replaced records may remain in historical snapshots or recovery copies stored by the app.

Deleting the app removes its local app data. To remove other copies, also delete any exported files, shared copies, and backups from the locations where they were saved, including Apple backups as appropriate. The developer cannot access or erase copies held on your device or in destinations you selected.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel it through Apple’s subscription settings.

6. Support and this website

The copy of this static website at normzeng.github.io/kitchen-margin-support/ is hosted by GitHub Pages and has no developer-added analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or cookies. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to host and secure the service, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Public marketing pages at kitchenmargin.blackvaletechnologies.com use Black Vale Technologies website analytics to measure visits and product interest. The Kitchen Margin app does not send kitchen records to website analytics. See the Black Vale website privacy notice for the data fields, retention periods, and controls, including how to turn off custom analytics.

If you choose to email support.kitchenmargin@blackvaletechnologies.com, the developer receives your email address and message contents to respond to your request. The app’s optional “Share usage diagnostics” export (Settings → About) produces a file with feature-use counts, event and installation timestamps, capture timings, time since last capture, coarse library-size ranges, and the app version. It contains no names, prices, photos, kitchen content, or direct identifiers. If you attach that file to support email, the developer receives the report and can associate it with your email address; it is not anonymous in that context. Nothing is ever sent automatically. Email providers process support messages to deliver and store them. We keep correspondence as needed to resolve and follow up on your request; you can request deletion by emailing the same address. Avoid sending sensitive records, and redact any information that is not needed to explain the issue.

If you choose to contact support through a GitHub issue, your GitHub username and everything you post are visible to the developer and to the public. We use that information to understand and respond to your request. Do not submit invoices, receipts, sales reports, backups, private business data, personal information, or payment information. Use fictional examples.

Public support discussions may remain available as a support record. You can edit your own issue content where GitHub permits, or request removal of a support post through the same support channel without reposting sensitive content. GitHub controls its own retention, account data, and platform copies under its policy.

7. Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app’s data practices change and show the effective date above. For questions about this policy, email support.kitchenmargin@blackvaletechnologies.com. Do not include private records that are not needed for your question.
